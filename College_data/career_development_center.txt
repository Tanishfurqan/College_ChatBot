CAREER DEVELOPMENT CENTRE (CDC)

The Career Development Centre (CDC) at New Horizon College of Engineering prepares students for successful careers through industry-oriented training, skill development, and placement support. The centre collaborates with industry partners to ensure students develop the skills and knowledge required for today's job market.

VISION
Enable Engineering and Management students to build successful careers in their dream organisations.

MISSION
• Provide opportunities for students to interact with industry professionals.
• Develop industry-relevant technical, professional, and life skills.
• Prepare students for placements through continuous training and guidance.

GOAL
Provide career guidance, life skills training, and placement opportunities to all students of New Horizon College of Engineering.

STUDENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

• Student Empowerment – Develop employability skills and encourage an entrepreneurial mindset.
• Continuous Learning – Promote lifelong learning and personal growth.
• Effective Communication – Strengthen communication, collaboration, and professional skills.
• Values & Identity – Build leadership qualities, teamwork, ethics, and professional values.

CAMPUS RECRUITMENT TRAINING (CRT)

The Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) programme prepares students for company recruitment processes through aptitude training, technical preparation, group discussions, and interview practice.

INDUSTRY INSTITUTE INTERACTION CELL

The Industry Institute Interaction Cell connects students with corporate professionals and helps them identify suitable career paths through industry interaction and career guidance.
