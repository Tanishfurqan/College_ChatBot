# Key Highlights & Achievements: New Horizon College of Engineering (NHCE)

## Institutional Status & Accreditations
- Permanent Affiliation: Permanently affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belgaum.
- Approvals: Approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education).
- NBA Accreditation: Awarded TIER-1 Accreditation by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
- NAAC Accreditation: Accredited by NAAC with an 'A' Grade.
- Academic Autonomy: Autonomous institution offering a cutting-edge curriculum, flexible academic policies, and Choice Based Credit System (CBCS).

## Rankings & Recognitions
- ARIIA Ranking: Recognized in the **"BAND – EXCELLENT"** category under *Colleges/Institutes (Private/Self-Financed) (Technical)* by the Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements (ARIIA), an initiative by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India.
- NIRF Engineering Ranking: Ranked **114th** across India as per NIRF 2020 Rankings (MHRD, Govt. of India) and **121st** in subsequent national engineering rankings.
- NIRF Private College Ranking: Ranked **4th** among the Top Private Engineering Colleges in Bangalore as per NIRF Ranking 2019.
- Tech School Rankings: Ranked **No. 1 Institute in Karnataka**, **6th in South India**, and **12th in India** among the "Best Private Tech Schools".
- AICTE–CII Rating: Ranked as a **Platinum Institution** by AICTE and the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII).

## Key Institutional Awards
- ASSOCHAM Award: Awarded as the **"Best Engineering College in India – South"** by ASSOCHAM.
- Industry Interface: Awarded for **"Excellence in Promoting Industry-Academia Interface"**.
- Global Exposure: Awarded as the **"Most Preferred Institute with Global Exposure"**.
- Employability Focus: Received the **"Best Institution towards Industry Focus Education Delivery for Full Employability"** Award.
- Global Collaboration: Received the honor for **Best International Education Collaboration of the Year**.

## Industry Collaborations & Academic Partnerships
- IBM Collaboration: Signed an MoU with IBM under the **New Horizon – IBM Global University Program**.
- Corporate Accreditations: Accredited by **TCS** and **Wipro** for their academic interface programs.
- Industry MoUs: Holds multiple MoUs with industries for campus connect, internships, and recruitments.
- Professional Memberships: Student and faculty memberships in professional societies such as ISTE, IIPE, IEEE, SAE, CSI, NGKC, and RAI.
- Emerging Areas: Offers **Minor Degrees in emerging areas** as initiated by AICTE.
- Value Addition: Offers various value-added courses alongside the regular curriculum.

## Placements & Global Opportunities
- Placement Record: Provides 100% placement opportunities for B.E., M.Tech, MBA, and MCA students in reputed companies.
- Japan Career Centre: Dedicated facility established for international placements and internships.
- Study Abroad Programs: Active Foreign University collaborations for study-abroad and student-exchange programs.
- Career Development: Regularly conducts national and international industrial visits, conferences, workshops, seminars, webinars, and guest lectures.

## Campus Infrastructure & Facilities
- Wi-Fi Enabled Campus: Fully Wi-Fi enabled infrastructure with modern learning facilities.
- Faculty-Student Ratio: Maintains faculty-student ratio in accordance with AICTE norms.
