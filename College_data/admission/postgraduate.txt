# Postgraduate (PG) Programs & Admissions: New Horizon College of Engineering (NHCE)

## M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering

### Duration
- 2 Years (4 Semesters)

### Eligibility Criteria
- Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or Technology in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) or Information Science & Engineering (ISE).
- GATE or PGCET score is mandatory.
- Admissions open to candidates from both Karnataka and outside Karnataka.

---

## Master of Business Administration (MBA)

### Duration
- 2 Years (4 Semesters)

### Eligibility Criteria
- Qualifying Degree: Passed Bachelor’s Degree with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks** across all years/semesters (rounding off percentage is strictly not permitted).
- Category Relaxation: Candidates from Karnataka belonging to SC/ST and Category-I require a minimum of **45% aggregate marks** across all years/semesters (rounding off percentage is strictly not permitted).

---

## Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

### Duration
- 2 Years (4 Semesters)

### Eligibility Criteria
- Qualifying Degree: Passed Bachelor’s Degree with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks** across all years/semesters (rounding off percentage is strictly not permitted).
- Prerequisite Subjects: Must have studied Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Computer Programming, Computer Applications, Business Mathematics, or Business Statistics as an optional/elective subject at the Degree level OR passed 10+2 / PUC with Mathematics/Statistics/Computer Science with 50% marks in that subject.
- Category Relaxation: Candidates from Karnataka belonging to SC/ST and Category-I require a minimum of **45% aggregate marks** in the qualifying degree or Pre-University subject.

---

## Application Procedure & Required Documents

### How to Apply
1. Fill out the application form online (`E-Application Form`) or download and submit the physical form.
2. Incomplete or false information will result in rejection of the application.
3. Candidates can also visit the Admission Office directly or contact admissions via phone/email.

### Documents Required for PG Admission
- 10th / SSLC Marks Card (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- 12th / PUC Marks Card (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- Degree Marks Cards (All Semesters / 3 Years - Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- Degree Certificate / Provisional Degree Certificate
- Transfer Certificate (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- Caste Certificate (For Karnataka SC/ST/Category-I candidates only)
- Migration Certificate (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies, for students from non-VTU / non-Karnataka boards/universities)
- Entrance Exam Scorecard (GATE / PGCET / KMAT / MAT as applicable)
- Photographs: 4 Passport size + 4 Stamp size
