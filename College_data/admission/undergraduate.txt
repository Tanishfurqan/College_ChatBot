## B.E. Admissions Guidelines

### Eligibility Criteria
- Qualifying Exam: Passed 10+2 / 2nd PUC / Equivalent examination with English as a language subject.
- Subjects Required: Minimum aggregate of **45%** in Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Bio-Technology / Computer Science / Electronics / Biology.
- Category Relaxation: SC/ST and OBC (Categories 1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B) candidates from Karnataka require a minimum aggregate of **40%** in optional subjects.

### Course Duration
- Duration: 4 Academic Years (8 Semesters).
- Semester Length: 16 weeks per semester.

### Admission Modes
1. Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET): Administered by Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) for Karnataka domiciles.
2. Management Quota: Direct admissions based on academic merit (minimum 45% aggregate score). Sports Quota scholarships are available for eligible athletes.

### Documents Required for Admission
- 10th / SSLC Marks Card (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- 12th / PUC Marks Card (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- Transfer Certificate (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies)
- Caste Certificate (For Karnataka SC/ST candidates only)
- Migration Certificate (Original + 2 sets of Photocopies, for non-Karnataka boards)
- Photographs: 4 Passport size + 4 Stamp size

## Department & B.E. Program Overviews

### Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- Established: 2001
- Accreditation: Accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
- Student/Faculty Strength: Motivated faculty team serving 700+ students.
- Innovation & Research: 31 patents filed by the department.
- Infrastructure: 16 in-house laboratories and 6 industry-sponsored labs (Cisco, Quest Global IIoT, Indo-French Center of Excellence, SAP, VMware IT Academy).

### Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML)
- Focus: Intelligent problem-solving, data analytics, and machine learning systems.
- Laboratories: Data Structures Lab, USP Lab, ADE Lab, OOP Lab, Microprocessor Lab, ADA Lab, DBMS Lab, Networks Lab, Java Lab, Mobile Application Development Lab, Web Technologies Lab, Software Testing Lab.

### Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Established: 2001
- Innovation & Research: 40 patents filed by the department.
- Research Divisions: Semiconductor Technologies, IoT-based Embedded Systems, Next-Generation Communication Systems, RF Power & Energy Systems, Advanced Signal/Image Processing.
- Industry Partners: IBM, HP, Texas Instruments, Sankalp Semiconductors, Audience Communication, Intel, ISRO, IISc.

### Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)
- Established: 2001
- Innovation & Research: 44 patents filed by the department.
- Laboratories: 10 in-house laboratories plus industry-sponsored labs (Indo-French Center of Excellence - IFCEEAE, Cisco Networking Academy, Quest Global, Schneider Electric, SAP Next-Gen Lab, HPE Vertica CoE, VMware IT Academy).

### Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Established: 2003
- Innovation & Research: 108 patents filed by the department.
- Specialized Laboratories: Heat Transfer Lab, Metrology & Measurement Lab, Machine Shop, Advanced Manufacturing Lab, CAD Lab, Metallography Lab, Design Lab, Fluid Mechanics & Machinery Lab, Foundry & Forging Lab, Workshop Lab, Energy Conversion Lab.
- Sustainability Initiatives: Installed wind turbines and solar panels on campus, fulfilling a significant portion of the college's energy needs.
